How the process works

Four steps from first contact to decision

We handle the process and follow up along the way – the decision is made by the authorities.

  1. 01

    First contact and clarifying your needs

    You fill in the form or write to us. We clarify position, scheme and timeline.

    You + us
  2. 02

    Document check and review of requirements

    We check contract, salary and terms against the requirements and send a checklist of the documentation needed.

    Us
  3. 03

    Preparing and submitting the application

    We complete and submit the application (AR1/AR6 or AR3/AR7) – with power of attorney where relevant.

    Us
  4. 04

    Follow-up and status until the decision

    We follow up with SIRI and keep you updated until the authority has made its decision.

    Us · decision: SIRI

Good to know: We are advisors/consultants, not an authority. The decision is made by SIRI, and we cannot guarantee outcomes or processing times. Biometrics and authority fees are handled at SIRI.

Process & responsibilities

Your part

  • Share contact details (name, email, company, phone).
  • Provide the employment contract/salary offer—or a brief case outline.
  • Approve our proposal to get started.

Our part

  • Validate documents and requirements—set the application up correctly.
  • Complete and submit the application, and follow up with SIRI (the Danish Agency for International Recruitment and Integration).
  • Provide updates until a decision/work permit is issued.
