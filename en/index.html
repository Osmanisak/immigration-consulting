Process & responsibilities

We handle the application, follow-up and dialogue with the authorities. The decision is made by SIRI.

Your part

  1. Share contact details (name, email, company, phone).

  2. Provide the employment contract/salary offer – or a brief case outline.

  3. Approve our proposal to get started.

Our part

  1. Validate documents and requirements – set the application up correctly.

  2. Complete and submit the application, and follow up with SIRI (the Danish Agency for International Recruitment and Integration).

  3. Provide updates until a decision/work permit is issued.
