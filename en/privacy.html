Privacy policy

How S.A. Group ApS processes personal data when you contact us or use our advice.

Data controller

S.A. Group ApS (CVR 46842944), Denmark, is the data controller for the personal data we receive through immigration-consulting.dk and in connection with our advice. Immigration Consulting is operated by S.A. Group ApS.

If you have questions about how we process personal data, contact us at info@immigration-consulting.dk or +45 71 34 18 35.

What we process

  • Contact forms: name, e-mail, company, phone and what you write in the message field.
  • Inquiries by e-mail and phone: the information you give us.
  • Advisory cases: the information and documents about the company and the employee that are needed to prepare and submit an application, such as terms of employment, salary and identity details.
  • Invoicing: the client's contact and billing details.

Please do not send passports, CPR numbers or other sensitive documents through the contact form. If we need them, we will agree on a secure channel.

Purposes and legal basis

  • To answer your inquiry and clarify whether we can help – GDPR Article 6(1)(b) (steps prior to entering into a contract) and (f) (our legitimate interest in answering inquiries from companies).
  • To deliver the agreed advice and submit applications – Article 6(1)(b) (performance of a contract) and (f).
  • To meet our obligations under the Danish Bookkeeping Act – Article 6(1)(c) (legal obligation).

Recipients and processors

We never sell personal data. The data may be shared with:

  • Netlify, which hosts the website and receives form submissions on our behalf. Netlify is a US company, and transfers to the US are based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • Our e-mail provider, which stores our correspondence with you.
  • The Danish Agency for International Recruitment and Integration (SIRI), when we submit an application on the client's behalf as agreed.
  • Our accountant and bookkeeping system in connection with invoicing.

How long we keep the data

  • Inquiries that do not lead to an engagement are kept only as long as needed to answer and follow up on the inquiry.
  • Data in an advisory case is kept for the duration of the case and afterwards only as long as needed to document the advice given.
  • Accounting records are kept for 5 years after the end of the financial year, as required by the Danish Bookkeeping Act.

Cookies and analytics

The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, marketing or tracking tools, so we do not show a cookie banner. Our hosting provider records technical information such as IP address and browser type in server logs to deliver and protect the website.

Your rights

You have the right to access the data we process about you and to have it rectified or erased. You can also object to the processing, have it restricted and in some cases receive your data (data portability). Contact us at info@immigration-consulting.dk to exercise your rights.

Complaints

You can complain about our processing of personal data to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, Denmark, datatilsynet.dk. We would, however, appreciate the chance to resolve the matter with you first.
