How we work

  1. Pre-analysis

    Contract check, salary requirements, scheme and timeline.

  2. Application

    Document collection; AR1/AR6 or AR3/AR7 submitted.

  3. Follow-up

    Dialogue with SIRI and ongoing status updates until decision.
